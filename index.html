Conference Group theory: Applications in analysis, geometry and arithmetic (IASM, Harbin, January 2027)

Group theory plays a prominent role in analysis, geometry, and number theory. This conference aims to bring together leading experts and young researchers to discuss recent developments in the field.

Time of the conference:

  • Some mini-courses (Profinite groups, Cohomology, Koszul/Massey vanishing properties, Bass–Serre theory ...): 4–8 January 2027
  • Conference: 11–15 January 2027
